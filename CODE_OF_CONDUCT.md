# Code of Conduct

PulseEngine's public beta is an engineering-feedback project. We want issues and discussions to stay useful to people with different experience levels, employers, tools and technical opinions.

Please:

- critique ideas, results and implementation behavior rather than people;
- provide reproducible evidence when possible;
- respect confidentiality and intellectual property;
- do not pressure anyone to disclose proprietary source, private repositories or customer information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam and deliberate disruption;
- assume that disagreement about a candidate or verdict is useful beta evidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that expose secrets/private information or make the project unsafe or unusable for others.

For serious conduct or security matters that should not be public, contact the maintainers privately rather than opening a public issue.
